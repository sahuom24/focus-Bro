FocusBro • Google Play submission • 1.2.0 (8)
Package: com.focusbro.myapp

Short description
Start a focus timer and choose which apps stay within reach.

Full description
Choose a session length, pick the apps you need, and start focusing. Save different app selections for study, travel, or your usual routine. Schedule recurring sessions or start one whenever you need it. Your timer and session history stay on your device.

Optional app restriction uses Android’s AccessibilityService API. During an active session, FocusBro checks the foreground app package and window class so it can recognize system prompts and bring you back to your timer when you open a restricted app. FocusBro does not read screen content or keystrokes, and does not send this information off your device. You can decline access and use the timer, or turn access off in Android Settings. Phone, messages, system settings and uninstall controls remain available.
