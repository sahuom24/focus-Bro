Privacy Policy for FocusBro (Break Mode)

Last Updated: November 25, 2026
Effective Date: November 24, 2026
Key Summary: FocusBro (com.focusbro.myapp) is 100% offline. It does not collect, track, or share any personal data. It does not even request the Internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET).

1. Overview and Core Privacy Principle

FocusBro is designed to help you regain focus and take mindful breaks without sacrificing your privacy. We believe your device usage and focus habits are entirely personal to you. Therefore:

  • No Data Collection: We do not collect, transmit, store remotely, sell, or share any personal information, app usage habits, or device identifiers.
  • No Internet Permission: The app does not declare or use android.permission.INTERNET. It cannot connect to external servers or transmit information off your device.
  • No Third-Party Trackers: No advertising SDKs, analytics frameworks, crash-reporting suites, or third-party trackers are included in the app.

2. Device Permissions & On-Device Processing

FocusBro requires specific permissions to operate its distraction-blocking and timer features strictly on your device:

Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

FocusBro includes an optional Accessibility Service (FocusAccessibilityService) used solely to:

  • Check the foreground app package and window class during a focus session. The window class identifies system security prompts that must remain available. When you open an app that was not selected for your break, FocusBro brings your FocusBro session screen back to the foreground to keep you focused.
  • User Control: Android System Settings and package installer/uninstall screens are never blocked, and you can grant, decline, or revoke this permission in Android Settings at any time.
  • Privacy Guarantee: The Accessibility Service does not read, store, or log any screen text, typing, passwords, or personal messages (canRetrieveWindowContent="false"). It operates entirely on-device and never shares data.

Notification Management

  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Displays the ongoing timer status and break completion notifications.
  • Do Not Disturb Access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY): Optionally silences incoming notifications during your focus session and restores your previous sound profile when the session ends. No notifications are intercepted or read.

Scheduling & Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)

Triggers user-scheduled focus sessions at your configured start time, even when the phone is in Doze mode.

Startup on Boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)

Restores active timers and scheduled focus sessions after your device restarts.

App Package Queries (<queries>)

Allows the app to display a local list of installed apps so you can choose which essential tools (e.g., Phone, SMS, Calendar, UPI) stay accessible during a break. This list is processed solely in local device memory.

3. Data Storage & Deletion

All settings, whitelist configurations, and session records are stored inside the app's local private sandbox (SharedPreferences). Device backups are disabled (allowBackup="false"). You can delete all app data instantly by uninstalling the app or clearing storage in device settings.

4. Children's Privacy

FocusBro is safe for users of all ages and does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13.
